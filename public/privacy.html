🌙 隱私政策

Privacy Policy
最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-06-18

一句話版本:你的所有資料只存在你自己的裝置上。我們沒有伺服器、沒有帳號、不收集、不上傳、不販售任何個人資料。 In one line: all your data stays on your own device. We have no server, no accounts; we do not collect, upload, or sell any personal data.

我們收集什麼資料

Moonyou 不會收集、傳輸或儲存任何個人資料到任何伺服器。你在 app 裡輸入的內容——包括月經日期、週期紀錄、斷食時數、基礎體溫(BBT)、分泌物觀察、目標設定與偏好——全部只儲存在你裝置上的瀏覽器本機儲存空間(localStorage)。這些資料永遠不會離開你的裝置。 Moonyou does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. Everything you enter — period dates, cycle records, fasting hours, basal body temperature (BBT), cervical mucus observations, goals, and preferences — is stored only in your device's local browser storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device.

帳號

不需要註冊,沒有帳號,不需要 email 或電話號碼。 No registration, no account, no email or phone number required.

行事曆匯出(選用)

如果你選擇使用「行事曆匯出」功能,Moonyou 會在你的裝置上產生一個 .ics 行事曆檔,由你自行加入 Google 行事曆或 Apple 行事曆。若你選擇連結 Google 行事曆,授權流程由 Google 直接處理,Moonyou 僅在你授權的範圍內、於你的裝置上建立行事曆事件,不會讀取或儲存你的行事曆內容到任何伺服器。 If you use the optional Calendar Export feature, Moonyou generates an .ics calendar file on your device for you to add to Google or Apple Calendar. If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the authorization is handled directly by Google; Moonyou only creates calendar events within the scope you grant, on your device, and never reads or stores your calendar contents on any server.

第三方

本 app 透過 Google Fonts 載入字型,並使用瀏覽器內建的快取機制以支援離線使用。除你主動使用行事曆匯出功能外,沒有任何分析工具、廣告、追蹤器或第三方資料分享。 The app loads fonts via Google Fonts and uses the browser's built-in caching for offline use. Apart from the Calendar Export feature you actively choose to use, there are no analytics, ads, trackers, or third-party data sharing.

資料的刪除與備份

因為資料只存在你的裝置上,你隨時可以透過清除瀏覽器資料、或解除安裝 app 來刪除全部資料。請注意:這也代表若你更換裝置或清除瀏覽器資料,資料將無法復原(除非你另行匯出備份)。 Because data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app. Note that this also means data cannot be recovered if you switch devices or clear browser data (unless you have exported a backup).

兒童

本 app 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計,亦不會故意收集兒童的任何資料(事實上我們不收集任何人的資料)。 This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children (in fact, we do not collect data from anyone).

健康免責聲明 / Health Disclaimer

Moonyou 僅供個人健康參考,不構成醫療建議,也不應作為避孕或受孕的唯一依據。如有健康疑慮,請諮詢專業醫療人員。 Moonyou is for personal reference only, does not constitute medical advice, and should not be relied upon as the sole method of contraception or conception. Consult a healthcare professional for any health concerns.
