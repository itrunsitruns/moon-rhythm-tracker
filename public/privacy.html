🌙 隱私政策

Privacy Policy
最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-11-27

一句話版本:你的所有資料只存在你自己的裝置上。我們沒有伺服器、沒有帳號、不收集、不上傳、不販售任何個人資料。 In one line: all your data stays on your own device. We have no server, no accounts; we do not collect, upload, or sell any personal data.

我們收集什麼資料

Moonyou 不會收集、傳輸或儲存任何個人資料到任何伺服器。你在 app 裡輸入的內容——包括月經日期、週期紀錄、斷食時數、基礎體溫(BBT)、分泌物觀察、目標設定與偏好,以及「諮詢」頁的健康問卷(目標、週期與病史摘要、用藥與補充品、飲食型態、禁忌事項)——全部只儲存在你裝置上的瀏覽器本機儲存空間(localStorage)。這些資料永遠不會離開你的裝置。 Moonyou does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. Everything you enter — period dates, cycle records, fasting hours, basal body temperature (BBT), cervical mucus observations, goals, preferences, and the health intake on the Consult tab (goals, cycle and medical history, medications and supplements, eating pattern, contraindications) — is stored only in your device's local browser storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device.

帳號

不需要註冊,沒有帳號,不需要 email 或電話號碼。 No registration, no account, no email or phone number required.

行事曆匯出(選用)

如果你選擇使用「行事曆匯出」功能,Moonyou 會在你的裝置上產生一個 .ics 行事曆檔,由你自行加入 Google 行事曆或 Apple 行事曆。若你選擇連結 Google 行事曆,授權流程由 Google 直接處理,Moonyou 僅在你授權的範圍內、於你的裝置上建立行事曆事件,不會讀取或儲存你的行事曆內容到任何伺服器。 If you use the optional Calendar Export feature, Moonyou generates an .ics calendar file on your device for you to add to Google or Apple Calendar. If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the authorization is handled directly by Google; Moonyou only creates calendar events within the scope you grant, on your device, and never reads or stores your calendar contents on any server.

健康問卷、諮詢摘要與預約(選用)

健康問卷只存在你的裝置上,可以隨時修改或在「諮詢」頁一鍵刪除。「產生諮詢摘要」會在你的裝置上組成一段文字,只包含你勾選的欄位;它不會自動傳送到任何地方,只有在你自己複製、下載或交給別人時才會離開你的裝置。 The health intake stays on your device; you can edit it or delete it with one tap on the Consult tab. "Create a consult summary" builds a text on your device containing only the fields you tick. It is never sent anywhere automatically; it leaves your device only if you copy, download or hand it to someone yourself.

「預約諮詢」會在新分頁開啟第三方預約服務。若你選擇附上摘要,摘要只會複製到你的剪貼簿,由你自己貼到預約頁面;Moonyou 不會把任何健康資料放進網址或自動傳送。你在預約頁面填寫的內容,依該服務的隱私政策處理。 "Book a consult" opens a third-party booking service in a new tab. If you choose to attach your summary, it is only copied to your clipboard for you to paste on the booking page; Moonyou never puts health data in a URL or sends it automatically. What you enter on the booking page is handled under that service's privacy policy.

第三方

本 app 透過 Google Fonts 載入字型,並使用瀏覽器內建的快取機制以支援離線使用。除你主動使用行事曆匯出或預約功能外,沒有任何分析工具、廣告、追蹤器或第三方資料分享。 The app loads fonts via Google Fonts and uses the browser's built-in caching for offline use. Apart from the Calendar Export and booking features you actively choose to use, there are no analytics, ads, trackers, or third-party data sharing.

資料的刪除與備份

因為資料只存在你的裝置上,你隨時可以透過清除瀏覽器資料、或解除安裝 app 來刪除全部資料;健康問卷也可以在「諮詢」頁單獨刪除。請注意:這也代表若你更換裝置或清除瀏覽器資料,資料將無法復原(除非你另行匯出備份)。 Because data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app; the health intake can also be deleted on its own from the Consult tab. Note that this also means data cannot be recovered if you switch devices or clear browser data (unless you have exported a backup).

兒童

本 app 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計,亦不會故意收集兒童的任何資料(事實上我們不收集任何人的資料)。 This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children (in fact, we do not collect data from anyone).

健康免責聲明 / Health Disclaimer

Moonyou 僅供個人健康參考,不構成醫療建議,也不是避孕醫療器材。 Moonyou is for personal reference only. It is not medical advice and not a contraceptive medical device.

  • 日曆法推估:排卵日與易受孕期是依你登記的月經第一天和平均週期長度推算(推估排卵日 = 週期長度 − 14)。實際排卵每月可能提早或延後,週期不規律時誤差更大。 Calendar-method estimates: ovulation and the fertile window are calculated from your logged period start and average cycle length (estimated ovulation = cycle length − 14). Actual ovulation can shift earlier or later each month, more so when cycles are irregular.
  • 不是避孕方法:Moonyou 不能取代保險套或其他避孕方法,也不能保證受孕。app 只以「可能較易受孕的幾天」「受孕機會較低的推估日」描述,不提供懷孕機率百分比;「較低」不等於零。備孕或避孕請與醫療專業人員討論。 Not contraception: Moonyou cannot replace condoms or other contraception and cannot guarantee conception. The app only describes "days you may be more likely to conceive" and "estimated lower-chance days", never a percentage; "lower" is not zero. Talk with a healthcare professional about conceiving or contraception.
  • 身體訊號是參考:蛋清狀分泌物通常出現在排卵前;基礎體溫通常在排卵後才上升,不能用來預測排卵。 Body signs are references: egg-white mucus usually appears before ovulation; basal temperature usually rises only after ovulation and cannot predict it.
  • 斷食:app 只提供最多 16 小時的「參考節奏」,是一般性的參考,不是處方。更長的斷食只能由使用者在設定裡設成自己的個人目標,且不適用於懷孕或備孕中、哺乳中、有進食障礙史、體重過輕、糖尿病或正在服藥、未滿 18 歲、肝腎疾病或正在接受癌症治療者;開始前請先諮詢醫師或營養師。 Fasting: the app only offers a "reference rhythm" of at most 16h, as general guidance, not a prescription. Longer fasts exist only as a personal goal the user sets in Settings, and are not for people who are pregnant or trying to conceive, breastfeeding, with a history of eating disorders, underweight, with diabetes or on medication, under 18, or with liver or kidney disease or in cancer treatment; talk to a doctor or dietitian first.
  • 今日小建議:由 app 依你的記錄套用固定規則組合,不是 AI,也不是個人化診療。真人諮詢不提供醫療診斷。 Today's little note: assembled by the app from your logs with fixed rules; it is not AI and not personalised medical care. The human consult does not provide medical diagnosis.

如有健康疑慮,請諮詢專業醫療人員。 Consult a healthcare professional for any health concerns.
